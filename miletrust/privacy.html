MileTrust

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

MileTrust ("we," "our," or "the App") is designed with privacy first. Your drives, routes, tax deductions, and receipt photos belong solely to you. We do not operate external tracking servers, do not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, and never sell or share your location history.

1. Data Stored On Your Device & Private iCloud

MileTrust does not require an account or login. All mileage logs, vehicles, odometer readings, saved places, frequent route rules, custom notes, and attached receipt photos are stored locally on your iPhone using Apple SwiftData. If you enable iCloud Encrypted Backup, your records sync privately between your own Apple devices through Apple's private CloudKit database, governed by Apple's iCloud security and privacy policies. We have no access to your private CloudKit container.

2. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

  • Location (Always & When In Use): Used solely to detect when a drive begins and ends, calculate accurate route distance, render trip maps, and match Saved Places or Frequent Route Rules. Coordinates are sent to Apple Maps (MapKit / CLGeocoder) only to resolve street addresses, map thumbnails, and road routes.
  • Motion & Fitness Activity: Used solely on your device to distinguish driving from walking/stationary states, reduce battery consumption, and audit Apple's hardware motion history for missed drives.
  • Camera & Photo Library: Used only when you explicitly attach a parking, toll, or odometer receipt photo to a trip record.
  • Notifications: Used only for local alerts you enable (such as missed-drive recovery alerts, evening review reminders, and tracking health watchdogs).

3. In-App Purchases

Optional MileTrust Pro subscriptions are processed directly by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit 2). We never receive or store your payment card details or Apple ID credentials.

4. Data Export, Control & Deletion

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • Export a portable JSON backup or IRS PDF/CSV report directly from the App.
  • Delete individual trips, vehicles, places, or route rules anytime, or erase all data via Settings → Data & Backup → Delete All Mileage Data.
  • Revoke Location, Motion, Camera, or Notification permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

5. Children's Privacy

MileTrust is intended for drivers and self-employed professionals and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as features evolve. Material updates will be reflected by updating the Effective Date at the top of this page.
