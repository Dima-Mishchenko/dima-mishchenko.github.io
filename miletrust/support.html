Automatic Drive Tracking

How do I get the most reliable background tracking?

Open Settings → Permissions & Sensors inside MileTrust and verify that:

  • Location is set to Always with Precise Location enabled.
  • Motion & Fitness is allowed so MileTrust can detect driving starts/stops and audit missed drives.
  • Background App Refresh is enabled and Low Power Mode is off during work shifts.

What if I ride a train, bus, or as a passenger?

Enable Car Bluetooth / CarPlay Only in Settings → Tracking Engine so MileTrust only starts automatic drives when connected to your car's Bluetooth or CarPlay.

What is Missed-Drive Gap Detection?

If iOS suspends background tracking or your phone battery dies during a drive, MileTrust audits Apple's hardware Motion & Fitness history and location discontinuities, then reconstructs the missing route distance with Apple Maps so you can review and recover it in one tap.

Classification & Tax Reports

Which IRS mileage rates does MileTrust use?

MileTrust automatically applies date-aware IRS standard mileage rates by trip date (including 2024, 2025, and 2026 H1/H2 splits), plus statutory Medical and Charity rates. Pro users can also set a custom employer reimbursement rate per mile.

What is included in MileTrust Free vs. MileTrust Pro?

  • Free: Unlimited automatic GPS drive tracking, swipe classification with Undo, missed-drive gap recovery, 1 vehicle, 1 frequent route rule, private iCloud backup, JSON backup/restore, and in-app tax report previews.
  • MileTrust Pro: IRS Schedule C, CPA Audit, TurboTax/QuickBooks, Employer Reimbursement, Expensify/Concur, and Excel/Sheets PDF & CSV exports, receipt photo appendices, custom reimbursement rates, multi-vehicle logs, and unlimited frequent route rules.

Subscriptions & Data

How do I restore, manage, or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed and managed through your Apple ID. You can tap Restore on the MileTrust Pro screen, or manage or cancel anytime in iPhone Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions (or via Settings → Manage Subscription inside MileTrust).

How do I back up or delete my data?

Go to Settings → Data & Backup to export or restore a portable JSON backup of your trips, vehicles, saved places, and route rules, or permanently erase all mileage data.
