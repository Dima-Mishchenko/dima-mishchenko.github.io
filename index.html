Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Capy iOS app.

Where can I download the app?

Capy is available directly on the official Apple App Store for iPhone. You can download and start tracking right away.

How does the AI food scanner work?

Our vision model analyzes your photo to recognize individual foods and estimate portion sizes. You can review the breakdown and adjust ingredients anytime with one tap.

Does Capy track micronutrients and vitamins?

Yes. In addition to calories, protein, carbs, and fat, Capy monitors over 25 micronutrients including dietary fiber, sodium, potassium, calcium, vitamin C, and more.

Is my personal dietary data private?

Absolutely. Your privacy is paramount. Your food logs and dietary metrics stay on your device or are securely synced. We never sell your personal information or dietary data.
