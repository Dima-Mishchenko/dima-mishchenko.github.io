Last Updated: October 13, 2026

Data Controller:

1. Privacy Principles

We believe your personal health and nutrition journey is deeply private. Calories Tracker is engineered with strict data minimization principles:

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your dietary data to advertisers or data brokers.
  • We process meal photos exclusively to estimate nutritional breakdown and discard raw images according to strict retention schedules.
  • We collect only the information essential to deliver our tracking, calculation, and support services.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with Calories Tracker, we may process:

  • Food & Dietary Logs: Photos of meals you submit for AI vision processing, food descriptions, portion sizes, meal times, and digestive symptom ratings (e.g. bloat notes).
  • Health & Profile Metrics: Optional profile information you choose to enter, such as height, weight, activity level, and dietary goals (e.g. maintenance, deficit, surplus).
  • Device & Usage Diagnostics: Device type, OS version, app version, language preference, and anonymous crash telemetry to fix bugs and improve stability.
  • Payment & Transaction Information: Purchases are processed securely via Apple App Store, Google Play, or our Merchant of Record partners (Paddle / Lemon Squeezy). We do NOT store your full credit card number or bank details on our servers.

3. How We Use Information

We use your information exclusively for:

  • Computing accurate calorie, macronutrient, and micronutrient calculations.
  • Providing intelligent insights into potential bloating triggers and digestive trends.
  • Syncing your preferences and managing subscription entitlements.
  • Responding to customer support inquiries and refund requests.
  • Preventing security abuse, spam, and fraudulent transactions.

4. Data Storage & Security

We employ industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS / TLS 1.3 in transit, AES-256 at rest) to protect your personal data. Access to backend systems is restricted to authorized personnel under strict confidentiality agreements.

5. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Under applicable international privacy regulations (including GDPR and CCPA), you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

  • Data Portability: Obtain your logged dietary data in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Opt-Out: Withdraw consent for non-essential processing at any time.
