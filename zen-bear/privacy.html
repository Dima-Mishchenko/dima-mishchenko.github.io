Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 18, 2026

At Zen Bear, accessible from our mobile application, your privacy is our top priority. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that are collected and recorded by Zen Bear and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

Zen Bear is designed with privacy in mind. We do not track, collect, or store any personal data or app usage details on remote servers.

  • Screen Time & App Activity: All app selection and shielding parameters requested via Apple's Screen Time framework (FamilyControls) remain strictly on your iOS device. Zen Bear does not have access to your personal app usage logs, web browsing history, or message content.
  • Focus Statistics & Preferences: Your study session durations, completed breaks, selected environments, and cat companion preferences are stored locally on your device via UserDefaults.

2. Use of Apple Screen Time API

Zen Bear utilizes Apple's official Screen Time API solely to restrict access to selected apps during active focus sessions. We never sell, transmit, or monetize your activity choices.

3. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

All financial transactions and subscription management are handled securely by Apple via StoreKit. Zen Bear does not collect or store your credit card or billing details.
