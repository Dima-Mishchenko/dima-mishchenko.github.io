Everything you need to know about Zen Bear: Cozy Pomodoro Focus & App Blocker.

1. Screen Time & App Shielding

How does distraction app blocking work?

Zen Bear uses Apple's official Screen Time API (FamilyControls & ManagedSettings). When a focus session begins, your selected distracting apps (e.g. social media, games) are automatically shielded to protect your deep focus.

Why aren't my chosen apps being shielded?

Please ensure Screen Time permissions are granted to Zen Bear. Go to iOS Settings > Screen Time > Zen Bear and make sure access is turned ON. Also verify that you have chosen specific app categories or apps under "Manage Apps" in the Zen Settings menu.

2. Subscription & Premium

How does the 3-day Free Trial work?

New users can try Zen Bear Premium free for 3 days. You will not be charged if you cancel in your Apple ID Subscription Settings at least 24 hours before the trial ends.

How do I restore my existing purchase?

Open Zen Settings inside the app, tap on "Zen Bear Premium", and select "Restore Purchases" at the bottom of the paywall. Your Apple ID subscription will automatically reactivate.

3. Customizations & Environments

How do I switch cozy environments or cat companions?

Tap the settings icon on the top right of the main study screen. Under **ENVIRONMENT**, choose between Day, Night, Rain, or Snow. Under **COMPANION**, toggle between Cozy Cat and Black Cat.
