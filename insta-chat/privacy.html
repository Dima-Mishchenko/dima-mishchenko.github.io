Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Anastasiia Debloat Instagram Assistant ("we", "our", or "the Service") handles information when you interact with @anastasiia.debloat on Instagram via Direct Messages or post/Reel comments.

1. Information We Process

When you send a Direct Message or comment on a post/Reel requesting a guide or resource, the official Meta Instagram API provides us with:

  • Your Instagram-scoped user ID, display name, and username (when available via Meta API)
  • The text content of your message or comment
  • Timestamps and event identifiers needed to prevent duplicate replies

2. How We Use Information

We use this information solely to:

  • Detect whether you requested a guide, resource, or link
  • Generate a personalized reply with your requested link (e.g., to debloat.space)
  • Maintain a brief operational log (up to the most recent 50 interactions) to prevent duplicate messages and troubleshoot delivery

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data. Message text and first name may be sent to Google Gemini API solely to draft the automated reply, and operational state is stored in private Google Cloud Storage.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Operational logs are automatically capped to recent interactions. You may request immediate deletion of any stored interaction data at any time by following our Data Deletion Instructions.

5. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please send a Direct Message to @anastasiia.debloat on Instagram or visit https://www.debloat.space/.
