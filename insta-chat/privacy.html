Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Soldatenko LLC ("we", "us") processes personal data when you interact with the Debloat 30 Instagram assistant on @anastasiia.debloat via Direct Messages or post/Reel comments, in connection with debloat.space.

1. Data We Collect & Process

When you send a Direct Message or comment on a post/Reel requesting a guide or resource, the official Meta Instagram API provides us with:

  • Your Instagram-scoped user ID, display name, and username (when available via Meta API)
  • The text content of your message or comment
  • Timestamps and event identifiers needed to prevent duplicate replies

2. How We Use Your Data

We process this data solely to:

  • Detect whether you requested a guide, resource, or link (such as the free 3-Day Debloat Kickstart)
  • Generate and send a personalized reply with your requested link to debloat.space
  • Maintain a brief operational log (capped to the most recent 50 interactions) to prevent duplicate replies and troubleshoot delivery

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use Instagram messaging data for third-party advertising.

3. Service Providers & Sharing

  • Hosting & Storage: Google Cloud Platform (Cloud Run & Google Cloud Storage), where the assistant runs.
  • AI Reply Drafting: Google Gemini API processes the incoming message text and first name solely to draft the automated reply.
  • Meta Platforms: Instagram Messaging and Comments APIs are used to receive and deliver messages.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Operational logs are automatically capped to recent interactions. You may request immediate deletion of any stored data at any time by emailing anastasiia@debloat.space or following our Data Deletion Instructions.
